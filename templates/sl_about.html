About the Sri Lankan Housing Model

This application uses a machine learning model specifically designed for the Sri Lankan real estate market. Unlike the Boston Housing model, this predictor takes into account local factors that significantly influence property prices in Sri Lanka, including proximity to Colombo, local amenities, and regional characteristics.

Why Sri Lanka-Specific?

Boston Model Issues

  • Based on 1970s US data
  • Different currency (USD vs LKR)
  • Different market dynamics
  • Irrelevant features (Charles River, etc.)
  • Different urban planning

Sri Lankan Model Benefits

  • Current market conditions
  • LKR currency predictions
  • Local factors (proximity to Colombo)
  • Relevant features (land size in perches)
  • Sri Lankan urban structure

Pricing Factors

Location Premium

Colombo and surrounding areas command premium prices due to business centers, amenities, and infrastructure.

Land Value

Land size in perches is crucial. Urban areas have smaller plots but higher per-perch values.

House Features

Modern amenities, parking, and house size significantly impact valuation.

Infrastructure

Proximity to good schools, transport, and commercial centers adds value.

Important Notes

  • Synthetic Data: This model uses realistic but generated data based on Sri Lankan market patterns
  • Currency: Predictions are in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR) with USD approximations
  • Market Dynamics: Considers current Sri Lankan real estate factors
  • Regional Variations: Location scores reflect actual market premiums across districts
  • Use Case: Educational and demonstration purposes - not for actual investment decisions

How to Use

  1. Navigate to the Predict page
  2. Select the appropriate location score for your area
  3. Enter property details in Sri Lankan context (perches, sqft, etc.)
  4. Rate amenities and access to facilities
  5. Get predictions in both LKR and USD
  6. Compare different scenarios using the comparison tool

Data Sources & Methodology

The Sri Lankan housing model was created using:

  • Market Research: Analysis of Sri Lankan real estate trends
  • Geographic Data: District-wise pricing patterns
  • Feature Engineering: Local factors like proximity to Colombo
  • Correlation Analysis: Relationships between features and prices
  • Random Forest Model: Captures non-linear market dynamics
